# Project Overview
## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### 1. Introduction

Retail Operations Excellence is a Business Analysis portfolio project developed around a fictional UK general merchandise retailer, Everyday Retail Group (ERG).

The project demonstrates how Business Analysis techniques can be applied to identify operational challenges, analyse existing processes, define business requirements and recommend improvements.

### 2. Organisation Profile

Everyday Retail Group (ERG) is modelled as a UK retailer operating:

- 25 physical retail stores.
- Approximately 1,200 employees.
- Multiple customer-facing and operational departments.

These are fictional scenario assumptions created for this portfolio project.

### 3. Business Challenges

The project explores four operational challenges:

1. **Returns and Refunds:** Improving return processing, refund controls and product inspection.
2. **Stock Availability and Inventory Management:** Improving stock accuracy, discrepancy investigation and product availability.
3. **Customer Complaints Management:** Standardising complaint recording, investigation and resolution.
4. **Price Accuracy and Shelf Label Management:** Improving price-change communication and shelf-label verification.

### 4. Project Objectives

- Analyse existing operational processes.
- Identify business gaps and improvement opportunities.
- Identify and assess relevant stakeholders.
- Define business and solution requirements.
- Develop business rules and user stories.
- Create a Requirements Traceability Matrix.
- Propose improved future-state processes.
- Develop an implementation roadmap.

### 5. Business Analysis Deliverables

The project includes:

- Business Analysis Case Study.
- Business Requirements Document.
- Stakeholder Analysis.
- Requirements Catalogue.
- Requirements Traceability Matrix.
- As-Is and To-Be Process Flow Diagrams.
- Implementation Roadmap.

### 6. Proposed Solution

The preliminary recommendation is to assess the feasibility of upgrading existing systems while improving operational processes.

An illustrative six-month implementation roadmap has been developed, subject to technical assessment and stakeholder validation.

### 7. Project Limitations

ERG is a fictional organisation. The business challenges, stakeholder assessments, current-state processes and proposed requirements are simulated and experience-informed.

No real stakeholder interviews, organisational approvals or implementation outcomes are claimed.

The requirements and proposed processes would require validation by relevant business stakeholders before implementation.
