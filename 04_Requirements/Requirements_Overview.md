# Requirements Management

## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### Purpose

This section contains the requirements documentation developed for the Retail Operations Excellence project.

The objective is to organise, define and trace the business and solution requirements across the four operational workstreams.

### Requirements Catalogue

The requirements catalogue contains:

| Requirement Category | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Business Requirements | 12 |
| Stakeholder Requirements | 17 |
| Functional Requirements | 30 |
| Non-Functional Requirements | 12 |
| Business Rules | 29 |
| User Stories | 9 |

### Requirements Traceability Matrix

The Requirements Traceability Matrix provides proposed links between business requirements, stakeholder requirements, functional requirements, business rules and user stories.

It also includes proposed verification activities.

### Project Status

The requirements and traceability mappings are initial analyst proposals based on a fictional business scenario. They have not been formally approved or verified by real stakeholders.
