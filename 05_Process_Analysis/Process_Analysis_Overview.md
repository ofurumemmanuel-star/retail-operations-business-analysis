# Business Process Analysis

## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### Purpose

This section documents the analysis of existing operational processes and the proposed improvements across the four project workstreams.

### Process Areas

1. Returns and Refunds
2. Stock Availability and Inventory Management
3. Customer Complaints Management
4. Price Accuracy and Shelf Label Management

### Analysis Approach

The project uses:

- Current-State (As-Is) Process Analysis
- Future-State (To-Be) Process Design
- Process Gap Identification
- Process Improvement Recommendations
- Operational Control Analysis

### Key Improvements Proposed

The future-state processes introduce clearer responsibilities, standardised recording, appropriate authorisation, improved escalation and verification controls.

### Supporting Document

`Process_Flow_Diagrams.pdf`

This document contains the proposed As-Is and To-Be process flows for all four operational areas.

### Project Status

The processes are simulated and proposed for a fictional organisation. They require validation by relevant business stakeholders before implementation.
