# Retail Operations Excellence
### Business Analysis Case Study | Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

## Project Overview
This portfolio project demonstrates the application of Business Analysis techniques to a fictional UK general merchandise retailer. The simulated organisation, Everyday Retail Group (ERG), is modelled as operating 25 physical stores with approximately 1,200 employees.

The project focuses on four workstreams:
1. Returns and Refunds
2. Stock Availability and Inventory Management
3. Customer Complaints Management
4. Price Accuracy and Shelf Label Management

## Business Problem
The simulated scenario explores potential inefficiencies in returns and refunds, stock accuracy and availability, complaint handling, and price-change execution. These issues could contribute to financial exposure, inconsistent processes, customer dissatisfaction and limited operational visibility.

## Objectives
- Analyse simulated current-state processes.
- Identify operational gaps and improvement opportunities.
- Identify and assess relevant stakeholders.
- Define business, stakeholder, functional and non-functional requirements.
- Document business rules and user stories.
- Create an initial Requirements Traceability Matrix.
- Propose improved future-state processes.
- Develop a preliminary solution recommendation and implementation roadmap.

## Business Analysis Techniques
Stakeholder analysis; Power–Interest Matrix; As-Is and To-Be process analysis; gap analysis; requirements elicitation and analysis; business rules; user stories and acceptance criteria; requirements traceability; solution options assessment; implementation planning.

## Proposed Solution and Roadmap
The preliminary recommendation is to assess the feasibility of upgrading existing systems and improving operational processes. The illustrative six-month sequence is: Month 1 preparation and validation; Month 2 returns and refunds; Month 3 inventory; Months 4–5 complaints and pricing; Month 6 testing, training and rollout.

## Project Status and Limitations
**ERG is fictional.** The organisation profile, business problems, stakeholders and As-Is processes are simulated and experience-informed. No real stakeholder interviews, client engagement, internal company data, approvals or implementation outcomes are claimed.

Requirements and traceability links are proposed initial drafts and require stakeholder validation. The solution recommendation and roadmap are preliminary and subject to technical, commercial and resource assessment. Numerical non-functional targets require validation.
