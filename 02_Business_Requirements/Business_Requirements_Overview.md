# Business Requirements Documentation

## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### Purpose

This folder contains the Business Requirements Document (BRD), which defines the business objectives, scope, requirements, proposed business rules, solution considerations and implementation expectations for the Retail Operations Excellence project.

### Main Business Areas

1. Returns and Refunds
2. Stock Availability and Inventory Management
3. Customer Complaints Management
4. Price Accuracy and Shelf Label Management

### Requirements Structure

The project requirements are organised into:

- Business Requirements
- Stakeholder Requirements
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- Business Rules
- User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

### Main Document

The Business Requirements Document provides the detailed explanation of the proposed business improvements.

**Project status:** Fictional case study. Requirements are proposed and require stakeholder validation.
