# Implementation Planning

## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### Purpose

This section presents the proposed implementation approach for the Retail Operations Excellence initiative.

It outlines the recommended solution direction, implementation phases, dependencies, risks and proposed success measures.

### Preliminary Solution Recommendation

The preliminary recommendation is to assess the feasibility of upgrading existing systems while improving operational processes.

This approach aims to improve operational controls, consistency and visibility without immediately replacing the entire technology environment.

### Implementation Roadmap

The proposed implementation is structured around six illustrative months:

| Period | Main Activities |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Preparation, requirements validation and technical assessment |
| Month 2 | Returns and Refunds improvements |
| Month 3 | Inventory Management improvements |
| Months 4–5 | Customer Complaints and Price Accuracy improvements |
| Month 6 | Testing, training and rollout |

### Key Dependencies

- Business stakeholder validation.
- Technical feasibility assessment.
- Requirements approval.
- Availability of relevant resources.
- Testing and training arrangements.
- Management support.

### Proposed Success Measures

- Return-related financial losses.
- Refund processing time.
- Inventory accuracy.
- Product availability.
- Complaint resolution time.
- Price discrepancy rate.
- Shelf-label verification completion.

### Project Status

The solution recommendation and implementation schedule are preliminary proposals for a fictional organisation. They are not approved implementation commitments. Costs, baselines, targets and delivery dates require further assessment.
