# Stakeholder Analysis

## Retail Operations Excellence — Everyday Retail Group (ERG)

### Purpose

This section presents the stakeholder analysis for the Retail Operations Excellence project.

The objective is to identify relevant stakeholder groups, understand their interests and influence, and establish an appropriate approach to stakeholder engagement.

### Stakeholders Identified

The project considers the following stakeholder groups:

1. Head of Retail Operations
2. Store Managers
3. Department Managers
4. Store Assistants
5. Customer Service Team
6. Inventory and Stock Control Team
7. Pricing and Promotions Team
8. Finance Department
9. IT Team
10. Customers

### Analysis Techniques

- Stakeholder Identification
- Stakeholder Register
- Stakeholder Interest and Influence Assessment
- Power–Interest Matrix
- Stakeholder Engagement Planning
- Requirements Elicitation Planning

### Proposed Elicitation Methods

- Interviews
- Observation
- Document Analysis
- Workshops
- Customer Feedback Analysis

### Project Status

The stakeholder analysis is based on a fictional organisation and simulated stakeholder assumptions. No real stakeholder interviews or organisational consultations are claimed.

The stakeholder register and influence assessments require validation in a real project.
